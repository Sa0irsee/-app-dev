# My Favorite Series: Partner for Justice

![Partner for Justice](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/0b/Partners_for_Justice.jpg)

## About the Series
**Partner for Justice** is a South Korean crime investigation and medical drama series. It follows a skilled forensic doctor and a determined prosecutor who work together to solve criminal cases.

## Main Characters
- **Baek Beom** - A talented forensic doctor who examines evidence to uncover the truth.
- **Eun Sol** - A passionate prosecutor who investigates cases and seeks justice.

## Why I Like This Series
I like *Partner for Justice* because it combines mystery, crime investigation, and forensic science. Every episode has interesting cases that keep me curious about what will happen next. I also enjoy how the characters work together to uncover the truth.
